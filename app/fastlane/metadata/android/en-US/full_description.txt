EasyTimer is a simple interval timer for workouts, stretching and focused work. Set an active duration, a rest duration and a number of sets, then follow the countdown with short sound cues. Zero sets repeats indefinitely.

Your last settings and volume stay on your device. No account or internet connection is needed.

EasyTimer is free software under GPL-3.0-only. Sounds and translations from Interval Timer by Matthew Nienow and contributors are used under the MIT licence.
